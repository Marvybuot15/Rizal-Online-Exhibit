01 / THE JOURNEY

A traveler’s eye.
A writer’s memory.

In 1891, Rizal experienced two nearby destinations with strikingly different atmospheres.

Hong Kong offered the energy of a busy, cosmopolitan port. Macau, by contrast, seemed quieter and more melancholy in his account. This exhibit presents the places, impressions, and historical context that help tell the story of his journey.

03 / REFLECTION

Places are more
than points on a map.

They are felt, remembered, and described.

Reading Rizal's impressions side by side reveals how setting can shape a traveler's experience. The contrast between Hong Kong's movement and Macau's quietness helps us read his journey not simply as geography, but as a record of observation.

As you explore, consider: How do a city's pace, architecture, and cultural history influence the way a visitor remembers it?
